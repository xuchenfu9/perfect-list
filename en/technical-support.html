How to create an portrait photo

  1. Take a photo or choose a clear portrait from your photo library.
  2. Select a photo size and background color.
  3. Adjust crop, retouching, and photo settings as needed.
  4. Preview, save to Photos, or use the print-layout option.

Features and photo tips

  • On-device AI background removal, background replacement, face positioning, and cropping.
  • Common photo sizes and print layouts; available sizes are shown in the App.
  • For the best result, use bright, even lighting, a simple background, and a sharp unobstructed face.

About the output

The App processes photos and does not create cards, certificates, credentials, or other official documents.

Privacy and contact

Your photo and face processing stay on your device; photos and face data are not uploaded. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
